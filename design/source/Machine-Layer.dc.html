Build, performance and contrast

  • Plain HTML first. Server-rendered pages; JavaScript only for the mobile menu. Hover effects are CSS.
  • Images. AVIF / WebP with width and height; lazy-load everything except the portrait. Screenshots are grayscale at rest, colour on hover.
  • Fonts. Self-host two families (League Spartan 800/900, Instrument Sans), font-display: swap; IBM Plex Mono only for placeholders and captions.
  • Motion. 200–320 ms slides and fills; prefers-reduced-motion removes all transitions.
  • Keyboard. Skip link, visible 3 px focus ring, hover effects mirrored on :focus-visible. Tap targets 44 px or larger on mobile.
