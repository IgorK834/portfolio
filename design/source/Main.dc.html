Spatial / Investment Parcel Analysis Platform

Domain
GIS · Polish spatial-planning data
My role
[TO BE FILLED — scope of my contribution]
Source
Private repository

A web application that automatically analyses the investment and planning context of a land parcel, using GIS and Polish spatial-planning datasets.

Analysis areas

  • Local spatial plans (MPZP)
  • General plans (POG)
  • OUZ and related planning data
  • Flood-risk data
  • Protected areas
  • Terrain / elevation
  • Infrastructure context
  • Map layers

Engineering highlights

  1. Vector tiles straight from PostGIS

    Mapbox Vector Tiles generated from PostGIS; WMS previews are proxied with tile caching; coordinate transformations handled explicitly.

  2. Provenance and data quality

    Explicit provenance, quality and freshness states, and a clear difference between missing data and a confirmed absence of a restriction.

  3. Deterministic, reproducible reports

    PDF reports built from immutable analysis snapshots, plus audit ZIP exports with hashes.

  4. MPZP extraction research Experimental R&D

    Exploring deterministic plus LLM-assisted extraction from MPZP documents. A research track, not a finished feature of the platform.
